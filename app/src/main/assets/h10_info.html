2. Electrode Placement & Signal Quality Tips

  • Moisten Electrodes: Lightly wet the rubber electrode areas on the reverse of the Pro Strap with water before snapping the sensor module in.
  • Strap Tightness: Adjust the strap so it sits snugly just below the chest muscles (sternum level) without causing discomfort.
  • Artifact Reduction: Accelerometer (ACC) data is continuously cross-referenced to score motion artifacts. Excessive body movement may introduce baseline wander.

3. Glossary of Terms

PVC Premature Ventricular Contraction: Early ectopic beat originating in the ventricles with wide QRS complex (>120ms) and compensatory pause.

PAC Premature Atrial Contraction: Early ectopic beat originating in the atria with normal narrow QRS width and incomplete pause.

AFib Atrial Fibrillation: Irregularly irregular RR interval pattern with absent discrete P-waves.

SDNN Standard deviation of all normal-to-normal RR intervals across the recording (overall HRV).

RMSSD Root mean square of successive RR interval differences (parasympathetic/vagal activity).

pNN50 Percentage of successive RR intervals that differ by more than 50 ms.
